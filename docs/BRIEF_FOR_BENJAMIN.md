# Brief for Benjamin — Divergences & Story Beef-Ups
*From Arinze & dev team, after reading Ch 1–7 + The Plan. Short version: the writing is strong, every choice resolves, the Admin escalation works. These notes are about making playthroughs feel personal — the "wait, that's not how mine went" effect — without forking your plot.*

**The one rule:** a divergence changes *one variable* and swaps *a few lines*. Never a parallel storyline. All paths still converge like you already write them.

---

## Divergences (7 flags, tracked in the ledger below)

**1. Who escorts Vi (Ch 5 → Ch 6).** You already offer Ayo / Loray / alone in Ch 5 — but Ch 6's perimeter sequence plays out with Ayo regardless. Make the danger happen to whoever the player sent. Same beats, different voice under pressure: Ayo goes silent, Loray jokes through fear, Vi alone is the scariest version. *Highest-value change on this list.*

**2. The anchor echoes (Ch 6 → Ch 7).** If the player chose "destroy one," the Ch 7 names-anchor is already reconfigured — Vi: "they adapted because of us." If untouched, it's pristine. Players will argue over whose fault the escalation was. That argument is the feature.

**3. Pitch's DM — secret or shared (Ch 4 → Ch 6).** Add one choice after Pitch's private contact: tell the group, or don't. If kept secret, when Kelvin later asks "is anyone else getting private contact?" the player is lying by silence in front of everyone. Three lines of cost, a season of guilt.

**4. The Admin quotes the player's own behavior.** It's studying them — let it show its work. Write 2–3 variants for ~5 Admin lines across the season, keyed to playstyle: silent types get `you watch more than you speak`; fast deciders get `you decide before you understand`. The game tracks this automatically; you just write the variants.

**5. Which fragment of Halima recovers (Ch 5+).** Tie recovered voice-memo seconds to the decrypt results. Same plot information either way — but one player hears her scared, another hears her certain. Write each fragment in two cuts.

**6. The undeleted message (Ch 6).** "Leave it there" is currently Loray's call — give it to the player. If they delete it, the Admin's next message arrives *pre-deleted*: `you don't like keeping evidence`. One line of payoff.

**7. Texting the dead thread (Ch 1 onward — the best one).** Halima's DM thread stays open all season. The player can text it. No reply — but read receipts appear, days late. When something finally answers in the endgame, players who kept texting get a different (worse) moment than players who went quiet. Write both versions of that scene.

## Beef-ups (no flags needed)

- **Case files live in the apps.** The bounty posting, village reports, Ayo's mission logs — currently told in chat. Put the documents themselves in the game's Files/Email apps as readables. No new plot; rewards diggers; makes the computer real.
- **More silence options.** "Stay silent" / "leave on read" as a third option wherever it fits. Cheapest option to write, fits the surveillance theme, and feeds #4 — passivity becomes a playstyle the Admin notices out loud.
- **One trust gate played out loud (Ch 7 fracture).** When "don't trust Kelv—" lands, the player's highest-trust character defends them by name; the lowest-trust one hesitates visibly. The hidden numbers become drama once, right before the endings cash them in. Write it as slots: [HIGHEST] and [LOWEST], we fill them at runtime.
